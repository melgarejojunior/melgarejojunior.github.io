This sample article shows how posts appear on the blog. You can edit or delete it before publishing your own writing.

## One idea at a time

Write in paragraphs. Use headings to organize sections and links to share references.

Markdown supports **bold text**, *italics* and lists:

- An observation.
- A reference.
- A question to explore further.

> A quotation can have its own space, too.

To add another article, create a file in `_posts` using the instructions in the README.
